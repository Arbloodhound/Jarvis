The Foo Fighters are an American rock band formed in Seattle in 1994. Founded as a one-man project by former Nirvana drummer Dave Grohl, the lineup currently consists of Grohl (lead vocals, guitar), Nate Mendel (bass), Chris Shiflett (guitar) Pat Smear (guitar), Rami Jaffee (keyboards), and Josh Freese (drums).
